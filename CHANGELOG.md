- Preserve existing equation-reference previews and batch heading demotion.
- Add English and Chinese onboarding, an illustrated feature overview, MIT licensing, a bug report template, and automated self-tests.

更新：新增功能菜单、全文独立公式整理、通用补充说明模板，并修复列表公式漏转和 NOTE 样式丢失问题。
